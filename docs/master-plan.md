# Master plan — Educational Gaming Platform

This is a boundary and backlog, not a reconstructed full original master plan.
The referenced conversation exposed the German MVP guide and schoolbook image;
its earlier assistant documents were unavailable as readable text.

Vision: an extensible educational gaming platform, with Animal Adventure as the
first English-learning vertical slice. Preserve the five-subject pathway:
mathematics, Spanish, English, natural science and social science.

Backlog after the first loop: 12 animals, three missions, matching, six shop items;
publisher-verified textbook/unit/skill mapping; additional game adapters; broader
subject content; separately designed accounts/synchronization if commissioned.
Rooms, pets and speech recognition require new product decisions.

Use the ISBN/source table and verification workflow in the MVP 1.1 plan. The
schoolbook photograph establishes a prospective curriculum, not verified unit
contents. Record source URLs, editions, access dates and rights before alignment.
Never grow the MVP merely because a future capability appears in this document.
